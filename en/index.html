About Us

Your trusted electronic components partner

Linpo Precision (Shanghai) Limited is a subsidiary of Linpo Precision Limited (Hong Kong), specializing in the distribution and sales of electronic components.

We represent multiple internationally renowned brands, with products spanning potentiometers, encoders, resistors, inductors, power devices, memory chips, conductive materials, and more — widely used in consumer electronics, industrial control, automotive electronics, and telecommunications.

We deliver one-stop electronic component solutions through expert technical support and efficient supply chain services.
