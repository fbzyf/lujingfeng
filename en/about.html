Company Profile

Linpo Precision (Shanghai) Limited is a subsidiary of Linpo Precision Limited (Hong Kong), headquartered in Xuhui District, Shanghai. We are a professional company dedicated to the distribution and sales of electronic components.

Leveraging our parent company's extensive industry resources and international operational experience, we have successfully secured authorized distribution for seven internationally renowned electronic component brands from the USA, Japan, Korea, and Taiwan — including BOURNS, HDK, KEC, NGI, Shin-Etsu, UTC, and WINBOND.

Our product portfolio covers potentiometers, encoders, resistors, inductors, power devices, memory chips, conductive materials, sensors, and more — widely applied in consumer electronics, industrial control, automotive electronics, telecommunications, new energy, and medical devices.

We are committed to being our customers' most trusted electronic components partner, delivering value through expert technical support, a stable supply chain, and efficient service.
