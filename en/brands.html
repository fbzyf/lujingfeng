BOURNS

USA  |  Established 1947

BOURNS is a global leader in electronic component manufacturing, headquartered in California, USA. The company is renowned for potentiometers, encoders, and circuit protection products, widely used in automotive electronics, industrial control, consumer electronics, and telecommunications. BOURNS is recognized worldwide for exceptional quality and innovative technical solutions.

Potentiometers Encoders Resistors Inductors Transformers Protection Devices

HDK (Hokuriku Electric)

Japan

HDK (Hokuriku Electric Industry Co., Ltd.) is a well-known Japanese electronic component manufacturer specializing in potentiometers and sensors. Its variable resistors are recognized for high precision and reliability, while its temperature and humidity sensors are widely used in industrial measurement and environmental monitoring. HDK delivers premium products to global customers through Japanese precision manufacturing.

Potentiometers Temperature & Humidity Sensors

KEC

Korea

KEC (Korea Electronics Co., Ltd.) is a leading Korean semiconductor manufacturer focused on power device R&D and production. Its product line includes power transistors, MOSFETs, diodes, and other power semiconductor devices, widely used in power management, LED drivers, and motor control. KEC has earned global customer trust through excellent value and consistent quality.

Power Devices

NGI (Nippon Graphite Industries)

Japan

NGI (Nippon Graphite Industries) is a Japanese specialist in carbon and conductive materials. With deep expertise in conductive inks, silver paste, and battery electrode conductive additives, its products are widely used in printed electronics, new energy batteries, and flexible circuits. NGI's unique material technologies support innovation across the electronics industry.

Conductive Inks Silver Paste Battery Electrode Conductive Additives

Shin-Etsu

Japan

Shin-Etsu Chemical Co., Ltd. is a world-leading manufacturer of chemical and electronic materials. In electronic interconnects, Shin-Etsu offers heat-seal connectors (zebra paper), conductive elastomers, touch switches, polymer conductive materials, and medical silicone catheters. With industry-leading material science, Shin-Etsu provides critical solutions for LCD displays, touch panels, and medical devices.

Heat-Seal Connectors (Zebra Paper) Conductive Elastomers Touch Switches Polymer Conductive Materials Medical Silicone Catheters

UTC (Unisonic Technologies)

Taiwan

UTC (Unisonic Technologies Co., Ltd.) is a well-known Taiwanese semiconductor design and manufacturing company specializing in power devices and analog ICs. The company offers a comprehensive range of power MOSFETs, voltage regulators, operational amplifiers, and more — widely used in consumer electronics, computer peripherals, LED lighting, and power management. UTC holds an important position in the global power device market with high quality and competitive pricing.

Power Devices

WINBOND

Taiwan  |  Established 1987

WINBOND Electronics Corporation is a global leader in semiconductor memory solutions, headquartered in Taiwan. The company specializes in the design and manufacturing of flash memory (NOR Flash / NAND Flash) and DRAM, widely used in IoT, automotive electronics, industrial control, and consumer electronics. WINBOND is renowned worldwide for innovative memory technology and reliable product quality.

Memory Chips
