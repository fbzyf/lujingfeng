2025-11-20
Shin-Etsu Conductive Materials Application Case Study

Shin-Etsu's heat-seal connectors and conductive elastomers continue to expand in LCD display panel applications, providing panel manufacturers with highly reliable interconnect solutions.

Read More →

Shin-Etsu's heat-seal connectors (zebra paper) and conductive elastomer products play a critical role in LCD display panel manufacturing.

As an electrical interconnect material between LCD panels and PCBs, zebra paper is the preferred choice for many panel manufacturers due to its exceptional conductivity and reliability. Shin-Etsu's conductive elastomer products are also widely used in touch panels, medical displays, and other applications.

If your products require high-quality conductive interconnect material solutions, please contact us for technical support and product samples.
